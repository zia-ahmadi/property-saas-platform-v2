What we implemented for Staff Roles and Permissions
1. Four membership roles: OWNER, ADMIN, MANAGER, and STAFF.
2. Permission definitions: Organization, membership, property, lease, billing, and maintenance permissions.
3. RolePermissionMapping: Defines which permissions each role receives.
4. OrganizationAuthorizationService: Validates organization membership, status, and permissions.
5. CurrentUserAuthorizationService: Connects the authenticated user to organization-level authorization.
6. Tenant isolation: Prevents users from accessing organizations they do not belong to.
